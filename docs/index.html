Anti-patterns in production AI, from RAG to advanced agentic systems

A field guide to the design mistakes that stall enterprise AI programs, why they fail, and the architectures that replace them. Covers retrieval, models, agents, operations, governance, the major cloud platforms, cost, and harness engineering.

Most enterprise AI programs don't fail because the model is weak. They fail because the system around the model was designed for a demo, not for production. That system covers data, retrieval, orchestration, guardrails, evaluation, identity and cost controls. This guide catalogs the recurring design mistakes across the full stack and pairs each one with a production-grade alternative.

Each entry is written to be defensible in an architecture review. It names a concrete enterprise scenario, the mechanism of failure, the replacement design, and the reason that design holds up under scale, audit and change.
